The 7 most common reasons SBA rejects WOSB applications

  1. Operating Agreement doesn't prove unconditional ownership and control
  2. Personal Net Worth math is calculated wrong (EDWOSB only)
  3. Missing or incomplete IRS Form 4506-T
  4. Ownership trace not documented across all entities
  5. "Daily management" language is fuzzy
  6. Voting rights are conditioned
  7. Spouse/family member listed in a way that triggers control questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does WOSB certification take?

If your documents are clean and your Operating Agreement language is right, SBA's stated timeline is roughly 90 days. With gaps, expect 6–9 months because every correction request resets the clock.

Do I need a lawyer to get WOSB certified?

Not necessarily. Many women-owned businesses self-certify successfully. A lawyer is helpful for complex Operating Agreement language, multiple-entity ownership structures, or trust-owned businesses. The workbook tells you when to consult one.

What's the difference between WOSB and EDWOSB?

WOSB requires 51%+ ownership and control by women. EDWOSB adds an "economically disadvantaged" requirement (Personal Net Worth under $850K, etc.). EDWOSB unlocks more contracts because EDWOSB-only set-asides exist on top of all WOSB set-asides.

Can I lose my certification?

Yes — through annual re-attestation lapses, ownership changes, or if you exceed economic-disadvantage thresholds (EDWOSB). The workbook includes a re-certification calendar to keep you compliant.
