About April Lewis

April Lewis is the founder and CEO of A.S. Business Consulting LLC — a Texas-certified leadership and workforce consultancy serving small businesses, government agencies, and Fortune 500 clients. ASBC is an SBA-certified Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and Economically-Disadvantaged WOSB (EDWOSB), Texas WBE and DBE, and a City of Austin HUB.

April holds OSHA, FEMA, and Texas Insurance certifications. She is also an Ordained Minister, Licensed Cosmetologist, Notary Public, Loan Signing Agent, and Process Server — credentials that give her a uniquely cross-disciplinary lens on workplace, leadership, and small business consulting.

She is the author of multiple printable workbooks on leadership development, OSHA compliance, government contracting, and SBA certification, and a sought-after speaker on first-time-manager development, sensitivity training, and small business growth.

Based in Round Rock, Texas. Reachable at april@choosesuccessnow.com or 512-589-4053.
